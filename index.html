How to read the project: Passenger counts are observed demand. Bus-stop distance and nearby-bus counts are access indicators. Scheduled travel time comes from timetable data. These measures identify areas for deeper investigation; they do not prove poor service or real walking time.

6. Plain-English Glossary

Station TypeTransfer Station means passengers can change between metro services. Standard Station means no transfer signal was identified in the dataset.
Metro CorridorThe metro route grouping used to compare different parts of the network.
Bus AccessEasy = under 250m from a bus stop. Moderate = 250–500m. Limited = over 500m.
Priority ScoreA screening score based on bus proximity, nearby bus coverage and metro connectivity. It is used to prioritise investigation.
GTFSA standard format for public-transport stops and timetable information. It is the technical data source.
Scheduled Travel TimeTimetable-based time between adjacent metro stops. It is not live or actual traffic travel time.
